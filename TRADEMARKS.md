This policy concerns branding only. Rights to use the OwlCTF software are
provided separately in [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE](NOTICE).

## Official marks

The protected marks include:

- The name “OwlCTF”
- The bracket-owl symbol
- The combined OwlCTF name and symbol
- Official logo and favicon variants distributed in
  src/OwlCTF.Web/wwwroot/images

## Uses allowed without separate permission

You may:

- Refer truthfully to the project as OwlCTF
- State that another project, service, or event is “based on OwlCTF”
- Link to the official repository and preserve the required footer credit
- Reproduce the name or an unmodified logo in reviews, articles, screenshots,
  tutorials, compatibility statements, and other descriptive material
- Redistribute unmodified official brand assets as part of an unmodified copy
  of OwlCTF
- Display the bundled branding when operating a substantially unmodified
  OwlCTF installation, provided you do not imply sponsorship or endorsement

These permissions are limited, non-exclusive, revocable, and do not transfer
ownership of any mark.

## Uses that require written permission

Without prior written permission, you may not:

- Use OwlCTF or a confusingly similar name as the primary name of a fork,
  hosted service, company, product, event series, domain, or social account
- Use an official mark in a way that suggests sponsorship, certification,
  partnership, or endorsement by the OwlCTF maintainers
- Modify, recolor, distort, animate, or combine an official logo to create a
  new primary brand
- Register or attempt to register an OwlCTF name, logo, domain, or confusingly
  similar mark
- Sell merchandise whose main value comes from an OwlCTF mark

Forks and materially modified services should adopt their own primary name
and logo. They may still describe themselves accurately as “based on OwlCTF”
and must retain the footer attribution required by [NOTICE](NOTICE).

Organizer-uploaded logos, sponsor images, event names, and other third-party
content are not OwlCTF marks. Their owners remain responsible for those
materials and any permissions needed to use them.

Questions or permission requests should be opened through the official
[OwlCTF repository](https://github.com/crypt0-wizard/OwlCTF).
